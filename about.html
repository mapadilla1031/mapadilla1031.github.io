About me

Hi — I am Marko Padilla. I graduated from the University of Texas at El Paso with a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science in May 2025. I like turning messy, manual work into reliable software: automation scripts, data pipelines, and small full-stack tools that people can actually use. I am detail oriented, comfortable debugging and reviewing code, and always interested in learning new platforms (including RPA and workflow tools) that help teams work faster with fewer errors.

Stack, work, and skills

Day to day I work most in Python and JavaScript, with SQL for data and reporting. I also use TypeScript, Java, and Bash when a project calls for it, and I build web pieces with HTML and CSS (like this site).

On the backend I have used FastAPI and Node.js for REST APIs, worked with PostgreSQL and MySQL, and packaged services with Docker. I am used to Git on GitHub, Linux-style environments, CSV ingestion, scheduled jobs, and thinking about tests, code review, and the software lifecycle the way you would on a real team — including Agile / Scrum from my capstone work.

Outside of class projects I have kept a role in logistics at FedEx Express since 2020, where I train new hires, stick to tight deadlines, and collaborate under pressure. That experience shaped how I write software: clear instructions, dependable delivery, and respect for operations people who depend on the tool working every time.

What I like to do

I enjoy building automation and analytics workflows — for example a delivery route performance platform where backend routines and SQL replaced recurring manual reporting and sped up route planning. I also liked my capstone, TRACE: a full-stack security testing tool with FastAPI, SvelteKit, and Neo4j, where we automated vulnerability checks and cut down repetitive analyst work.

I like learning in public: reading docs, stepping through bugs, and using modern AI-assisted development tools thoughtfully (as a boost to engineering, not a shortcut past understanding). If you want the fuller picture, my project write-ups and code live on GitHub, and I am on LinkedIn as well.
