About

I am a computer scientist focused on automation, data pipelines, and full-stack tools that people actually use every day. I graduated from the University of Texas at El Paso with a B.S. in Computer Science in 2025 and have been happiest when I can turn messy operational workflows into something stable and observable.

Most of my recent work sits at the intersection of internal web apps, SQL-backed reporting, and integrations — the kind of software that keeps trucks, warehouses, and customer service aligned when the day gets loud.

Outside of work I like reading codebases end to end, tightening types and schemas, and small projects that make repetitive clicks disappear.
